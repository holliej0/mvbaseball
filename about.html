About MVBC

Founded in 2025, Mountain Valley Baseball Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in the Long Valley area. We were created to give young athletes another opportunity to play organized baseball, improve their skills, and grow as players.

Our
Mission

Mountain Valley Baseball Club is dedicated to providing instruction, mentoring, and coaching to youth through baseball programs. We strive to promote teamwork, leadership, and sportsmanship among young athletes while offering opportunities for youth development through organized baseball teams.

Who We Serve

The organization primarily serves youth athletes in the Long Valley, Morris County, NJ area.
